## results that differ from expectation

- a: with the 0.90 exclusion, 12 ReLU and 6 Softplus network-task combinations are dropped, almost all DNMS; DNMS pairs keep only 1 ReLU network (net 7). Applied per network (as in Yang et al. 2019) the rule would leave 1 ReLU network. Every result below is unchanged in sign and significance from the 0.80 version.
- b: the anti-diagonal arm is 1.2 % of unit-pairs; their task-1 preference flips between halves in 55 % of cases (5 % elsewhere) and they are weakly selective. Only 0.56 % of unit-pairs are TI in one half and TD in the other. Requiring a consistent preference sign in both tasks keeps 88 % of unit-pairs (81 % of TD), removes the arm (r 0.88 → 0.98) and leaves the category pattern intact (TD > TI where the response changes, p = 0.002).
- f: refitted inside each network; all 40 network x category slopes are positive (p = 0.002 per category, the floor with 10 networks). The old MixedLM p-values are withdrawn.
- g: no noise level reproduces the monkey three-point. Where TI-only matches the FEF value (0.80, σ ≈ 2.35) the model gives TD-only 0.48 (data 0.32) and random 0.64 (data 0.60): noise moves the model along one curve and the FEF values lie off it. The mouse values (−2 to −1 s window) lie near the curve (where TI-only matches, σ ≈ 2.2: TD 0.49 vs 0.50, random 0.67 vs 0.74); without noise the mouse-like TD-only readout is 0.17, so near-chance TD-only in the mouse is consistent with reversing TD units diluted by noise. Right plot: a partly shared pursuit output does reach FEF: best g = 0.62 (fully shared g = 1 is the Figure 5 set-up and overshoots the transfer).
- h: the thresholds are fixed from the final networks, so the categories average 20 % only at the end. Between categories the spread narrows over training (% TD 30 → 22 points, % TI 22 → 12) while the ordering is present from the first checkpoint.
- d, e: Softplus networks reproduce the ReLU pattern in every category.
